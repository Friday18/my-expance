## Full description
My Expance is the quickest way to track what you spend.

TALK TO LOG
Type "spent 450 on lunch" or "uber 220 yesterday" and it's logged in the right category. Ask "how much on food this month?" and get a real answer from your own data.

SEE YOUR MONTH SIX WAYS
Arc, Donut, Bar, Bubbles, Jar and Classic. Pick the one that makes sense to you. Tap a colour to see just that category.

STAY ON BUDGET
Set a monthly budget and the jar shows what's left, plus a safe amount to spend each day.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Delete your account and everything in it any time from Settings.

## Privacy answers

### Google Play — Data safety
| Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose | Optional |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Email address | Yes | No | Account management | No |
| User IDs | Yes | No | Account management | No |
| Other financial info (expenses you enter) | Yes | No | App functionality | No |
| Other user-generated content (assistant messages) | Yes, processed | No* | App functionality | Yes |
| Crash logs, diagnostics | Yes | No | Analytics (stability) | No |

\* Sent to an AI model provider (e.g. Groq) only to generate replies; service providers don't count as "shared".
- Data encrypted in transit: **Yes**
- Users can request deletion: **Yes** (in app + deletion URL)

### Apple — App Privacy
- **Contact Info → Email Address:** linked to user, app functionality, not used for tracking
- **Identifiers → User ID:** linked to user, app functionality
- **Financial Info → Other Financial Info:** linked to user, app functionality
- **User Content → Other User Content:** linked to user, app functionality
- **Diagnostics → Crash Data:** not linked to user, app functionality
- **Tracking:** No
